## General
I participated in Alberta Game Jam 2026, which was a **48-hour** in-person jam! We started with a group of 4, ultimately ending up with a group of 5, with the list of roles as follows:
### Credits
 - Malachi Eben-Munro (BluffPenguin): Main Programmer
 - Shaq Rodney Velasquez (Gxph): Programmer
 - Jie Fan (Teravortryx): Music and SFX
 - Mike D. (VRDev): Artist
 - Me (Leichii): Artist and Producer

A special thanks as well to Lite, without them we wouldn't be able to have done the diversifier "Can I Borrow This"!

Speaking of which, here were the diversifiers we successfully completed:

### Diversifiers
1. Don't Forget my Close-up!
  - Include the Albertosaurus
2. "Can I Borrow This?"
  - Use an asset (art, audio, or otherwise) from another team in our game
3. Unspoken Rule
  - Create a game where the rules are not communicated through words
4. Hustle Culture
  - Create a game where the player never stops moving
